TRANSPARENT & EDITABLE

Prioritisation methodology

Draft rules for operational triage, separate from official JMP service ladders. Recent water unavailability can trigger an urgent priority regardless of score. Otherwise the capped score combines the evidence below.

Method, interpretation and limitations
  1. Keep historical vulnerability, current assessments and rainfall evidence as separate layers.
  2. Use canonical province + community IDs; join only exact approved keys or explicit crosswalks. Do not fuzzy match names silently. Match school and facility records through their community key while retaining sector identity.
  3. Use observation date, not sync date. Records beyond the freshness window require reassessment. Unknown water availability triggers verification.
  4. Score = recent no-water weight + rainwater-main weight + matched historical shortage weight + fresh area rainfall deficit weight, capped at 100. Rainfall deficit means the 3-month ratio is below the editable threshold.
  5. Urgent override first, then High and Watch thresholds. Stale observations take precedence as Needs update; future dates beyond one day are not recent.
  6. Rainfall and ENSO provide context. These reports cannot by themselves attribute a particular shortage to El Niño. JMP national estimates are context, not village-level risk scores.
  7. Confirm delivery feasibility, existing assistance, vulnerable groups and verified population before allocating resources. No population totals are inferred from mixed survey units.

Browser edits are local scenarios. To change team defaults, export rules and commit the reviewed file to docs/data/methodology.json. Imports and exports contain no credentials.

PROVENANCE & DATA READINESS

Sources and live connections

Connect incoming evidence

All five supplied Kobo APIs feed the Tikina analysis through an aggregate-only workflow every 15 minutes, subject to GitHub scheduling delays. Individual submissions are processed in memory and are not published. The site checks the deployed aggregate every 60 seconds while open. An optional KOBO_TOKEN secret is needed only if the form APIs become restricted. This is scheduled near-real-time, not a streaming feed.
